MIDDLE EAST

Houthi drone hits Riyadh's tallest tower again — 11 injured

RIYADH — A drone struck the 80-storey PIF Tower in Riyadh's financial district, its second hit in under a week. Saudi Civil Defence said 11 people were hurt. The Saudi-led coalition answered with strikes on Yemen's Hodeidah port, and the Houthis threatened Red Sea ports.

STRAIT OF HORMUZ

Iran says two tankers hit mines in Hormuz

Tehran's Revolutionary Guard said two tankers exploded and caught fire after striking naval mines in the strait early on Wednesday. The claim is unverified; attacks on shipping near Hormuz are at their highest since February.

SPACE · CHINA

Shenzhou-24 lifts off with first Pakistani astronaut

China's Shenzhou-24 mission carried a Pakistani astronaut to the Tiangong space station — the first foreign national to fly aboard a Chinese crewed spacecraft.

CHINA · POLITICS

Shanghai party chief Chen Jining under graft probe

Beijing's anti-corruption watchdog is investigating Politburo member and Shanghai party secretary Chen Jining — the most senior official felled in the biggest party shake-up in decades.

TECHNOLOGY · US TRIAL

US jury convicts Huawei of stealing trade secrets

A US federal jury convicted China's Huawei in a sprawling criminal trial over trade-secret theft and its dealings with Iran, capping years of legal battles.

COLOMBIA

Gunmen seize 25 hostages in supermarket siege

Armed men took 25 people hostage inside a Colombian supermarket after a failed raid on an armoured cash truck, as police surrounded the building.

VIETNAM

Suspected gas explosion in Hanoi kills six

A suspected gas explosion tore through a building in Vietnam's capital, Hanoi, killing six people and injuring ten, Vietnamese authorities said on Wednesday.
